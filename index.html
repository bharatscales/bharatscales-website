FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Common questions about AI automation software, industrial IoT, and predictive maintenance for smart factories.

What is AI automation software for manufacturing?
AI automation software uses machine learning and rules-based logic to automate operational decisions on the plant floor — from anomaly detection and predictive alerts to guided workflows for operators. BHARATSCALES connects to live IT, OT, and edge data so decisions are based on real-time conditions, not stale reports.
How do industrial IoT solutions improve factory operations?
Industrial IoT solutions connect sensors, PLCs, historians, and enterprise systems into a unified data layer. BHARATSCALES gives teams real-time visibility across assets and sites, enabling faster troubleshooting, better OEE tracking, and a foundation for predictive maintenance and manufacturing analytics.
What does predictive maintenance software do?
Predictive maintenance software analyzes sensor and equipment data to forecast failures before they cause downtime. BHARATSCALES scores asset health, triggers alerts, and integrates with CMMS systems so maintenance teams act on condition — not arbitrary schedules.
What is a smart factory in Industry 4.0?
A smart factory uses connected devices, AI, and real-time analytics to automate and optimize production. BHARATSCALES provides the platform and AI agents — including Smart Factory, Digital Twin, and Predictive Maintenance agents — to operationalize Industry 4.0 at scale.
How is manufacturing analytics different from traditional reporting?
Traditional reporting shows historical data in static dashboards. BHARATSCALES manufacturing analytics contextualizes live data across lines and sites, surfaces anomalies automatically, and connects insights to actions — whether that's a maintenance work order, an operator alert, or a production adjustment.
Is BHARATSCALES suitable for Indian manufacturing environments?
Yes. BHARATSCALES is built for India's industrial sector with edge-to-cloud architecture, vendor-neutral integrations, and 25+ field-tested deployments across manufacturing, chemical, mining, and logistics — without requiring full infrastructure replacement.
